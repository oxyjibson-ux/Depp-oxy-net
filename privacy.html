🔒 Privacy Policy ya Depp Net

Iyi Privacy Policy isobanura amakuru Depp Net ishobora gukoresha n'ingene tuyafata mu gihe ukoresha application.

1. Amakuru y'abakoresha

Mu gihe wiyandikisha, Depp Net ishobora kubika amakuru akenewe kugira konti yawe ikore, nk'izina, username, email hamwe na User ID ya Firebase.

2. Posts na Comments

Iyo ushyize Post canke Comment, ayo makuru ashobora kubikwa muri database kugira ngo abandi bakoresha babone ibiri ku rubuga.

3. Chat

Ubutumwa wohereza biciye muri Chat bubikwa kugira ngo bushobore kuboneka kuri konti yawe n'uwundi muntu muganira.

4. Notifications

Depp Net ishobora kubika notifications zijanye na Likes, Comments n'ibindi bikorwa kugira ngo ushobore kubimenyeshwa.

5. Umutekano w'amakuru

Dukoresha serivisi za Firebase mu gufasha kubika no kurinda amakuru ya application. Naho dufata ingingo zo gukingira amakuru, nta system ishobora kwemeza umutekano 100%.

6. Gusangira amakuru

Ntukoreshe Depp Net mu gusangiza amakuru y'ibanga y'uwundi muntu ata ruhusha. Amakuru ushyira ku mbuga rusangi ashobora kubonwa n'abandi bakoresha.

7. Amahitamo yawe

Urashobora gukoresha Settings kugira uhindure ibintu bimwe na bimwe bijanye na Notifications, Online Status, ururimi na Dark Mode.

8. Guhindura Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy ishobora guhindurwa igihe Depp Net yongeye ibintu bishasha canke ihinduye uburyo ikora. Version nshasha niyo izokoreshwa.

9. Kwemera Privacy Policy

Mu gukoresha Depp Net, uba wemeye iyi Privacy Policy n'uburyo amakuru asobanurwa muri iyi page ashobora gukoreshwa.

📅 Itariki

Privacy Policy ya Depp Net yatanguye gukoreshwa muri 2026.
