📜 Amategeko ya Depp Net

Aya mategeko asigura ingene ukoresha Depp Net n'ibintu wemera igihe ukoresha iyi application.

1. Gukoresha Depp Net

  • Ugomba gukoresha konti yawe bwite kandi ukarinda amakuru yawe yo kwinjira.
  • Ntukoreshe Depp Net mu bikorwa bitemewe n'amategeko.
  • Ntukagerageze kwinjira muri konti y'uwundi muntu ata ruhusha.
  • Ntukoreshe application mu guhemukira, gutera ubwoba canke guhungabanya abandi.

2. Posts n'ibindi usangiza

Uri wewe ujejwe ivyo ushyira kuri Depp Net. Ntushireko ibintu bibangamira abandi, ibintu bitemewe n'amategeko, canke ibintu vyotera ingorane abandi.

3. Chat n'ubutumwa

Ubutumwa bwo muri Chat bugomba gukoreshwa mu buryo bubaha abandi. Ntukoreshe Chat mu gutera ubwoba, guhohotera, ubusuma canke ibikorwa bitemewe.

4. Konti

Ugomba gutanga amakuru y'ukuri igihe wiyandikisha. Niba ubonye ikibazo kijanye n'umutekano wa konti yawe, fata ingingo zo kuyikingira.

5. Abakoresha bashobora gukumirwa

Depp Net ishobora kugira uburyo bwo gukumira umuntu canke guhagarika ibikorwa bibangamira abandi bakoresha.

6. Umutekano

Ntusangize abandi password yawe, code zo kwinjira canke amakuru y'ibanga. Depp Net ntizokubaza password yawe biciye mu butumwa busanzwe.

7. Guhindura aya mategeko

Aya mategeko ashobora guhindurwa igihe Depp Net yongeye ibintu bishasha canke ihinduye uburyo ikora. Iyo hari amahinduka, amategeko mashasha niyo azokoreshwa.

8. Kwemera aya mategeko

Mu gukoresha Depp Net, uba wemeye gukurikiza aya mategeko n'ibisabwa kugira application ikoreshwe mu buryo bwiza kandi butekanye.
